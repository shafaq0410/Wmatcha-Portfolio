Welcome to WMATCHA&CO.

From Wazuka to the World

WMATCHA & CO. is not a company that merely sells matcha. It is a company that reimagines culture, industry, and spirituality—through tea. Rooted in KANECHU, our ancestral tea house in Wazuka, Kyoto, we exist to return matcha to the center of global culture as a force of meaning, not mere consumption. We gather those who refuse compromise—those who choose clarity over noise, substance over hype, and purity over convenience.

About Us

Why hasn't an iconic matcha brand emerged from Japan?

In gaming, there is Nintendo. In automobiles, Toyota. In sake, Dassai. Across industries, Japan has created brands that transcended their categories and became global standards. They did not merely export products—they exported values. But when it comes to matcha, something feels unfinished. This question is the beginning of WMATCHA & CO. Founded from an unresolved tension between Japan's cultural depth and matcha's loss of voice in global markets, we exist to restore what modern life takes away.

Our Journey

Philosophy

W - Wazuka

Wazuka & Wa - Harmony

W stands for Wazuka, a land where tea has never been an industry alone, but a way of life. It also represents wa—the circle that connects people—and harmony itself. From this sacred ground, we carry forward a lineage preserved across generations.

M - Maitreya

Renewal & Restoration

M refers to Maitreya, the future Buddha—a symbol not of religion, but of renewal, restoration, and purity of heart. What if matcha could restore what modern life takes away? Not as stimulation, but as a culture that returns people to themselves.

Magokoro (忠)

Sincerity & Wholehearted Devotion

"Chu" (忠) can also be read as Magokoro — sincerity, or wholehearted devotion. As I care for the tea fields entrusted to me by previous generations, I continue to reflect on how a life lived with sincerity should be expressed through tea, and how those leaves can flow beautifully into society.

Our Initiatives

Brand

WMATCHA & CO. - Wholesale & Culture

A company engaged in wholesale and cultural initiatives, dedicated to creating an iconic Japanese tea brand that exports values, not just products. Leading a new era through tea, from our ancestral tea house KANECHU in Wazuka, Kyoto.

Platform

WTEA - Direct Trade Revolution

The world's first direct trade platform dedicated to Japanese tea. WTEA enables direct transactions starting from just one kilogram, restoring fairness by removing non-producing middle layers. Producers regain ownership of their work, pricing, and future.

Impact

Industry Transformation

Reshaping the structure of the tea industry by supporting aging farmers, creating transparent pricing in an inflating market, and connecting those who protect the diversity of Japanese tea. True tea lovers do not speculate. They participate.

Company

Heritage

KANECHU · Wazuka, Kyoto

The roots of WMATCHA&CO. trace back to KANECHU (カネ忠), a family tea house once tended by Toshi's ancestors in Wazuka, Kyoto.

After studying and working abroad, Toshi rediscovered the value of Japanese culture through the eyes of the world. An interest in matcha grew into a commitment — to preserve tea-growing communities, and to carry forward the legacy entrusted to his generation.

Today, WMATCHA&CO. tends its own fields in Wazuka while learning cultivation directly from leading producers, seeking not only to inherit tea culture, but to build a future in which it continues to thrive.

Our Why

Mission

How can Japanese tea survive for the next hundred years?

While global demand for matcha continues to grow, tea-producing regions face aging farmers, labor shortages, abandoned fields, and a dwindling number of successors. At the same time, consumers grow ever more distant from the people and places behind what they drink.

We believe tea is more than a commodity. It is the result of generations of knowledge, craftsmanship, and stewardship of the land.

Our mission is to reconnect producers and consumers — building a future where tea is valued not only for its flavor, but for the people, culture, and landscapes behind it. Through WMATCHA and WTEA, we work toward a more transparent, sustainable, and narrative-driven tea industry.
